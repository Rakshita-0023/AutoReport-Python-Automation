# AutoReport | Business reporting automation

If your team rebuilds the same Excel report every week or month, AutoReport can turn that workflow into a repeatable run. We agree on which records count, how to handle exceptions and what the finished report should show. The workbook gives managers a consistent view and gives the team a traceable list of source corrections.

**Minimum project fee ₹5,000.** The synthetic demo illustrates the workflow. Client work is scoped against your actual export layout and reporting rules.

## Delivery and handover

1. Review redacted sample files together and agree on required fields, duplicate rules, exclusions and one set of expected results.
2. Deliver the workbook and a runnable workflow. Test it against the agreed sample and show how to read an error or correct an excluded record.
3. Hand over the source, run instructions and acceptance results. The client names the person who owns future exports and report runs.

## Optional maintenance · from ₹2,000/month

Covers one monthly health check of the agreed workflow and up to one hour of troubleshooting or minor adjustments to the existing layout or rules. Additional hours and new scope are quoted separately. Hosting, on-call coverage and new integrations are outside this plan.
